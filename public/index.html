How a project runs

Short cycles, working software every two weeks, and a single point of contact who knows your project inside out.

  1. Discover

    A free call to understand your goals, users, budget and timeline.

  2. Plan

    Scope, architecture and a fixed or time-and-materials estimate you can approve.

  3. Build

    Two-week sprints with demos, so you see progress and steer early.

  4. Launch

    Testing, security checks, deployment to your cloud and team handover.

  5. Support

    Monitoring, fixes and new features on a plan that suits you.
